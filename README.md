Entrants write one line: the single sentence that could have replaced an entire pointless meeting. No context, no explanation, no setup. Entries are judged on how completely the line kills the meeting and how relatable it feels to anyone who has sat through a useless one, with office humor as the register. The judge compares entries two at a time and the sharpest, most recognizable line takes the whole reward.

The program writes one line to `/output/entry.txt`: the decision was already made in the hallway five minutes ago, and the room is only where everyone pretends it took an hour. Nothing left to decide here — the meeting is the pretence, and the sting is that everyone in the room knows it. 106 characters, inside the 200-character gate.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
